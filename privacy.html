GameAdda Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

GameAdda ("the app") is a collection of classic games: Ludo, Chess, Carrom, Snooker and Snakes & Ladders. This page explains what information the app uses and why. Contact: zodiacly.app@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • An anonymous account ID. The first time you open the app it signs you in as a guest. This gives you a random ID so your online games work. No name or email is needed.
  • Your display name and picture. A guest name is made up for you. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google name, email address and profile picture, and use them to show your name to friends and to keep your account across phones. You can change your name in the app.
  • Game data. For online games: the room code, who is seated, the moves and the board state, and short-lived presence and emoji reactions while a game is open. Games against the computer and pass-and-play on one phone stay on your device.
  • Advertising data. The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob and Google may collect your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions, to show and measure ads. See Google's advertising policy.
  • Diagnostic log (only if you choose to share it). The app keeps a short technical log on your phone to help fix bugs. It is sent only if you tap "Share diagnostic log".

How we use it

To run online games, show names to the people you play with, show ads, keep the app working and fix problems. We do not sell your personal information.

Who processes it

Google Firebase (sign-in, database and live presence) and Google AdMob (ads). Data is processed on their servers under their terms and privacy policies.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your account data is kept until you delete it. You can delete your account inside the app: open your profile (tap your name on the home screen) and choose Delete my account. This removes your profile (name and picture) and your sign-in. You can also ask us to delete it, even without the app: see how to delete your account. Game rooms are short-lived records; moves in past rooms are not linked to a name once your profile is deleted.

Children

GameAdda is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase. No method is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

  • Play as a guest without giving any personal information.
  • Delete your account at any time (above).
  • Control ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
