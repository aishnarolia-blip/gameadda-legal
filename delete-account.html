Delete your GameAdda account

You can delete your GameAdda account and the data linked to it at any time.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open GameAdda.
  2. On the home screen, tap your profile (your name at the top).
  3. Tap Delete my account, then tap it again to confirm.

This removes your display name, picture and sign-in straight away.

Option 2: by email (no app needed)

Email zodiacly.app@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my GameAdda account". Send it from the email address you used to sign in with Google. If you only ever played as a guest, tell us your guest name. We will delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted: your display name, profile picture, Google sign-in link and guest ID.
What may remain: records of past game rooms, which no longer show any name; and data that Google AdMob has collected, which is governed by Google's privacy policy.
